Nine README files by seven authors were analysed as exemplars of front-door writing. Reading them shaped the
guidance on opening order, tables of contents, badges, humor, help escalation, and the "Features" list. Every
finding in `references/exemplars.md` is expressed independently, and nothing was copied.
