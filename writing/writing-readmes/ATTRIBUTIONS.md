## Influencing Sources

The four README files below were analysed as exemplars of front-door writing. Reading them shaped this skill's
guidance: the demo-before-install ordering, the treatment of admonitions as skippable asides, the
`--help`-shaped options block, the honest-limit redirect, and the identification of a bullet-list "Features"
section as the section most likely to answer no reader question. Every finding in
[references/exemplars.md](references/exemplars.md) was expressed independently from observation of these
documents; none reproduces their wording or structure.

- <https://github.com/Azure-Samples/serverless-chat-langchainjs> — `README.md`
- <https://github.com/Azure-Samples/serverless-recipes-javascript> — `README.md`
- <https://github.com/sinedied/run-on-output> — `README.md`
- <https://github.com/sinedied/smoke> — `README.md`

Exemplars were supplied by the skill's author as examples of front doors that read well. Analysis covered
structure, opening, prose and tone, formatting conventions, reader-orientation, and weaknesses.
