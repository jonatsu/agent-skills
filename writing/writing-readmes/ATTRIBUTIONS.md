## Influencing Sources

The nine README files below, by seven independent authors, were analysed as exemplars of front-door writing.
Reading them shaped this skill's guidance: the demo-before-install ordering, the trigger conditions for a table
of contents, badges, a centered masthead and raw HTML, the rule that humor belongs only in motivating prose,
the tiered help escalation as a closing, the anticipated-objection dialogue, the `--help`-shaped options block,
the honest-limit redirect, and the identification of a bullet-list "Features" section as the one most likely to
answer no reader question. Every finding in [references/exemplars.md](references/exemplars.md) was expressed
independently from observation of these documents; none reproduces their wording or structure.

- <https://github.com/Azure-Samples/serverless-chat-langchainjs> — `README.md`
- <https://github.com/Azure-Samples/serverless-recipes-javascript> — `README.md`
- <https://github.com/sinedied/run-on-output> — `README.md`

Exemplars were supplied by the skill's author as examples of front doors that read well, several with the
author's own stated reasons for preferring them. Analysis covered structure, opening, prose and tone,
formatting conventions, reader-orientation, and weaknesses.
