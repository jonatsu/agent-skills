## Anthropic, "Prompting best practices"

- Publisher: Anthropic
- Source:
  <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices>
- Read: 2026-09-28
- Source license: none stated on the page; not relied on
- Relationship: idea-level influence, independently expressed

The guide informed the receiver framed as a capable colleague who lacks the writer's context and the test of
reading the prompt without that context; giving the reason behind a constraint so the model generalizes; wording
that separates acting from suggesting; separating instructions, context, examples, and input, with long material
before the ask; varied, marked examples; stating the target behavior rather than a prohibition; matching the
prompt's style to the output wanted; and calm emphasis, because forceful wording makes current models over-apply a
rule.

- Source license: MIT

The source informed the advice-only stance for improving a user's prompt, checking a draft for missing context
such as acceptance criteria, scope boundaries, and conventions to follow, asking at most three questions before
rewriting, delivering a diagnosis with the rewritten prompt in one fenced block and the reason for each change, and
replying in the user's language. Its component catalogue, model table, intent and scope tables, and examples are
not retained.
