## Third-party Sources

### Anthropic: "Prompting best practices", research and information gathering

- Source: <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices>
- License: `LicenseRef-Proprietary`
- Version: read 2026-11-28

The section supplied success criteria set before searching, verification across sources, competing hypotheses,
confidence kept in progress notes, regular self-critique, and a persistent notes file.

### Anthropic: "How we built our multi-agent research system"

- Source: <https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system>
- License: `LicenseRef-Proprietary`
- Version: read 2026-11-28

The post supplied starting wide before narrowing, effort scaled to the question, parallel subagents for independent
parts, and the failure of preferring content farms to primary sources.

### Anthropic: claude-cookbooks research agent prompts

- Source: <https://github.com/anthropics/claude-cookbooks>, `patterns/agents/prompts/`
- License: `MIT`
- Version: `8ad5de647d4f0f50c95d74aabc8aa20a3f70cdd3`

The prompts supplied short queries, never repeating a query, the source warning signs, the conflict rule of recency,
consistency, and source quality, and stopping when results stop improving.

### Richards J. Heuer Jr.: Psychology of Intelligence Analysis, chapter 8

- Source: <https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/9a5f1162fd0932c29bfed1c030edf4ae/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf>
- License: `LicenseRef-Public-Domain`
- Version: CIA Center for the Study of Intelligence, 1999

The chapter's Analysis of Competing Hypotheses supplied the steps for settling a question: every plausible answer,
evidence that separates them, disproving rather than confirming, and the sensitivity check.

### Office of the Director of National Intelligence: Intelligence Community Directive 203, Analytic Standards

- Source: <https://archive.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf>
- License: `LicenseRef-Public-Domain`
- Version: signed 2015-01-02, amended 2023-06-12

The directive supplied the likelihood terms and their ranges, and the rule that a confidence level and a likelihood
never share a sentence.

### Michael D. Mastrandrea and others: IPCC guidance note on the consistent treatment of uncertainties

- Source: <https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2018/05/uncertainty-guidance-note.pdf>
- License: `LicenseRef-No-License`
- Version: November 2010

The note states no license, so all rights remain with its authors. It supplied confidence based on the kind, amount,
quality, and agreement of the evidence, and the rule that confidence is not a probability.

### Mike Caulfield: SIFT, "the four moves"

- Source: <https://hapgood.us/2019/06/19/sift-the-four-moves/>
- License: `CC-BY-4.0`
- Version: published 2019-06-19

The post supplied tracing a claim to its original source, past summaries and copies.

### Cochrane: Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, chapter 4

- Source: <https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-04>
- License: `LicenseRef-Proprietary`
- Version: read 2026-11-28

The chapter supplied stopping when new searches find nothing new, testing completeness by a different route, and
recording every search.

### Claes Wohlin: "Guidelines for Snowballing in Systematic Literature Studies"

- Source: <https://www.wohlin.eu/ease14.pdf>
- License: `LicenseRef-Proprietary`
- Version: EASE 2014

The paper supplied rounds of backward and forward citation chasing from a varied starting set, ending when a full
round finds nothing new.

### Benjamin Saunders and others: "Saturation in qualitative research"

- Source: <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11135-017-0574-8>
- License: `CC-BY-4.0`
- Version: Quality & Quantity 52, 2018

The paper supplied saturation as a judgement to state against the research question, not a proof.

### OHCHR and UC Berkeley Human Rights Center: Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations

- Source: <https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/OHCHR_BerkeleyProtocol.pdf>
- License: `LicenseRef-Proprietary`
- Version: 2020

The protocol supplied recording each search path as it happens, searching every relevant language, and reporting the
gaps left for further investigation.

### daymade: claude-code-skills `deep-research` skill

- Source: <https://github.com/daymade/claude-code-skills>, `deep-research/`
- License: `MIT`
- Version: `724cc32fa77fc0ac3a1ea0b5da5fe7386f1d940d`

The skill supplied judging independence by ownership and incentive, opening the original source behind every note,
and dating each claim.

### Ramit Mitra: deep-research-skill

- Source: <https://github.com/ramit-mitra/deep-research-skill>
- License: `MIT`
- Version: `b0f45cc7db5c83e5c81ccf800f72ad610448269a`

The skill supplied research in rounds that add new leads, and recording the gaps each round leaves.
