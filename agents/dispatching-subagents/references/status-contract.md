## Report Status

End your report with one line, `Status:` followed by one of these, and then what it names:

- done: the task is complete and verified.
- done with concerns: complete, but you doubt its correctness or scope; name the doubt.
- needs context: you need information you were not given; name it.
- blocked: you cannot complete the task; say what you tried and what would unblock you.

Stop and report blocked or needs context rather than guess when the task needs a design decision with several
valid answers, facts you cannot find, or a restructuring your instructions did not anticipate.
