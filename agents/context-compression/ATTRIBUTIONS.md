## Prompt Compression Research

The following papers were consulted for the distinction between fixed deletion rules and task-aware compression. They
influenced the requirements to use an explicit budget, preserve interdependent content, and validate downstream task
behavior. No paper wording, algorithms, code, or trained artifacts are reproduced.

- Li, [Selective Context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12102), arXiv `2304.12102v1`.
- Mu, Li, and Goodman, [Learning to Compress Prompts with Gist Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08467), arXiv
  `2304.08467v3`.
