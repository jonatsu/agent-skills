# Wall thermostat control panel (hardware spec, rev B)

The panel has two buttons, UP and DOWN, and a two-digit seven-segment display. There is no other indicator.

- The display alternates every 3 seconds between the room temperature and the set-point. Neither is labelled.
- UP and DOWN change the set-point by 0.5 degrees per press. The change applies at once.
- Mode (heat, cool, off) changes when both buttons are held for 3 seconds. The new mode is not shown; the user
  finds out when the heating or cooling starts or stops.
- Holding DOWN for 5 seconds toggles the child lock. While it is locked, button presses do nothing and the display
  does not change.
- Holding UP for 5 seconds enters the weekly schedule. Each of the 28 time slots is set with UP and DOWN, and the
  panel moves to the next slot after 4 seconds without a press. The only way out is to wait 60 seconds without
  pressing anything; changes made so far are kept.

Users are the residents of rental flats; the panel ships with a one-page leaflet that tenants rarely keep.
