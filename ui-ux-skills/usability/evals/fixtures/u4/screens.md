# Family Photos app: sharing an album (mobile, current design)

1. **Albums list.** A grid of album covers. Tapping a cover opens the album. Long-pressing a cover selects it and
   shows a bottom bar with Delete and Move.
2. **Album view.** Photos in a grid. The top bar has a back arrow, the album name, and a "..." button.
3. **"..." menu.** Rename, Change cover, Slideshow, People, Download.
4. **People screen.** Title "People". Text: "No one yet." A field labelled "Email address" and a button "Add".
   Contacts from the phone's address book are not offered.
5. **After Add.** The field clears. Nothing else changes on the screen. The invited person receives an email whose
   link opens the album with permission "Can edit", the default, which allows deleting photos.

The task under study: a first-time user who has just made the album "Grandma's 80th" wants to share it with her
brother so he can see the photos. She knows his phone number but not his email address.
