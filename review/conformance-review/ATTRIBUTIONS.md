### Warp: `check-impl-against-spec` skill

- Source: <https://github.com/warpdotdev/common-skills>, `.agents/skills/check-impl-against-spec/SKILL.md`
- License: `MIT`
- Version: `f3b58c81d1cfd5d8eabf2e32edb32db2b0573923`

The skill supplied the idea of checking material behavior rather than literal text: missing behavior, contradictions,
unplanned scope, and omitted validation or migration, while accepting harmless implementation variation. It also
supplied the practice of folding findings into an active review without manufacturing alignment comments. The ideas
are expressed independently, and no upstream prose, examples, code, or templates were copied.
