## Third-party Sources

### Matt Pocock: `domain-modeling` skill

- Source: <https://github.com/mattpocock/skills>, `skills/engineering/domain-modeling/SKILL.md` and
  `skills/engineering/domain-modeling/CONTEXT-FORMAT.md`
- License: `MIT`
- Version: `959a8e9f1edc3adbe2f7e3054bb6fbefa6696260`

The skill supplied the active modeling discipline (challenge conflicting terms, sharpen overloaded ones, stress-test
relationships with edge cases, and cross-check claims against code), inline capture of resolved vocabulary, a
vocabulary-only glossary, and lazy creation of single-context or multi-context files. `references/glossary-format.md`
adapts the glossary entry shape from `CONTEXT-FORMAT.md`. This package adapts and rewords the material, and its worked
terms are independent.
