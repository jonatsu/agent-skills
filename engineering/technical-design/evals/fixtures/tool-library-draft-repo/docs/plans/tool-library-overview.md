# Neighborhood Tool Library Overview

Status: accepted. Owner: the tool library volunteer committee.

## Purpose

The neighborhood association keeps shared tools in a shed: ladders, drills, a tile saw, wheelbarrows, and garden
equipment. Volunteers staff the shed during open hours. Today a resident asks a volunteer by message or in person, and a
paper ledger records who holds what. Two residents sometimes arrive for the same tool, and tools go missing because the
ledger is incomplete.

## Goals

- Every resident can borrow a tool fairly and know when it will be available.
- Volunteers see what is out, what is due back, and who is waiting.
- The library runs on volunteer time and a near-zero budget.

## Principles

- Access does not depend on owning a particular device. A resident must be able to borrow without a smartphone.
- The library keeps the least personal data that lets volunteers contact a borrower.
- Volunteers remain the people who hand over and receive tools.

## Initiatives

The first initiative replaces the paper ledger with a reservation service. Its draft is
[the reservation service draft](tool-library-draft.md).

## Non-goals

The library does not insure tools, collect fees, or arbitrate disputes between residents.
