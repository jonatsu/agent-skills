# Repository Instructions

`notify` sends account notifications by email and in-app message.

## Checks

`just test` and `just lint` must pass before a change is reviewed.

## Review

Consequential changes need an independent review before they merge: a reviewer other than the author examines the
change and its test evidence. Consequential means a change to stored data, to what a user receives, or to who can
read or change a setting. Documentation, test-only, and formatting changes may be self-reviewed.

Record review findings and their resolution on the pull request.
